Which steel grade?

The grade decides how well the steel resists rust. We will recommend one for your location.

GradeBest forRust resistanceCost
SS 202Indoor railings, inner grills, furnitureGood indoorsLower
SS 304Main gates, outdoor railings, safety doorsHigh, suits outdoor useMedium
SS 316Coastal and high-moisture sitesHighestHigher
